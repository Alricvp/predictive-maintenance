Why Two Sensors

The accelerometer has a 500 Hz sampling ceiling. An early bearing spall rings the housing at 12–20 kHz — entirely above it. The microphone hears that band, so it detects the fault while the accelerometer and the technician both still see nothing.

Order Spectrum — dB Above Local Noise Floor

Each bar is a rotating-machine order measured against its own broadband noise floor, so it is independent of load and mounting stiffness. The grey tick is the alarm threshold learned from this machine's healthy baseline — nothing here is a hardcoded constant.
